# QuizCraftQA Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** March 26, 2026
_Last updated: March 26, 2026_
**Version:** 1.0.0

QuizCraftQA ("we," "our," or "us") is an ISTQB exam preparation web app that turns PDF study materials into adaptive quizzes. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles your data.

## Information We Collect

**QuizCraftQA does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.**

- No user accounts or registration required.
- No cookies or tracking.
- No analytics or telemetry.
- No personal information of any kind.

## PDF Processing

- PDF files you upload are processed entirely in your browser using client-side JavaScript.
- No PDF content is ever sent to any server.
- Files are discarded from memory when you close or refresh the page.

## How We Use Your Information

All functionality runs locally in your browser. No data is sent to any external server.

## Storage and Retention

- No data is stored beyond your current browser session.
- Closing or refreshing the page clears all data.

## Data Security

- All processing happens locally in your browser.
- No network transmission of your data occurs.

## Children's Privacy

QuizCraftQA is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect new features or legal requirements. Continued use after changes constitutes acceptance.
