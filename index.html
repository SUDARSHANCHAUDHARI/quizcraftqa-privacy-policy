QuizCraftQA Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 17, 2026

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Version: 1.3.0

QuizCraftQA ("we," "our," or "us") is a web app. QuizCraftQA is a browser-based ISTQB exam-prep and PDF-to-quiz tool that stores study data locally in the browser. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. By installing or using QuizCraftQA you agree to the practices described below.

Information We Collect

Location Data

  • QuizCraftQA does not collect location data.

Account Information

  • No account exists. There is no sign-up, no sign-in and no server of ours to sign in to.

Data Stored in Your Browser

  • PDF text you import, generated quiz questions, your answer history and your settings are stored in your browser's IndexedDB, under three keys: quizcraftqa-uploaded-pdfs, quizcraftqa-question-history and quizcraftqa-settings.
  • Your PDF is parsed on your device. The PDF reader (pdf.js) is bundled into the app and runs in a web worker in your browser. The file is never uploaded, not to us and not to anyone else.
  • We receive none of it. There is no Sudarshan Tech Labs server for this app.

How We Use Your Information

Location Sharing

  • QuizCraftQA does not use your location.

What the App Does

  • Reads the PDFs you choose, extracts their text, and builds quizzes from it — all in your browser.
  • Keeps your question history and settings in browser storage so you can continue later.
  • Shows a dashboard of your accuracy and per-topic progress, computed on your device.

Service Improvement

  • The app contains no analytics, no crash reporting and no tracking of any kind. No Google Analytics, no gtag, no tag manager, no third-party SDK.
  • There is a "Telemetry" toggle in Settings, and its name is misleading enough to be worth explaining: turning it on only starts an in-app event log you can read in the app yourself. It lives in memory, holds at most the last 200 events, is cleared when you turn the toggle off or reload the page, and is never written to storage or sent anywhere. Nothing reaches us whether it is on or off.
  • We do not sell, rent or monetize your study data. We never receive it in the first place.

Storage and Retention

Data Storage

  • PDF-derived content, quiz history and settings are stored in your browser's IndexedDB on your own device.
  • The app is an installable progressive web app, so a service worker also caches the app's own files (HTML, scripts, styles, icons) for offline use. That cache holds the program, not your study data.
  • No Sudarshan Tech Labs server is used for any part of QuizCraftQA.

Data Retention

  • Browser-local data remains until you delete it in the app, clear the site's storage, or reset your browser profile.
  • Clearing site data for this site removes both the IndexedDB study data and the service worker's cache of the app files.

Data Security

  • Your study data sits in your browser's own storage for this site, protected by the browser's same-origin policy: another website cannot read it. The app adds no encryption of its own, so anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile can read it.
  • The published app is served over HTTPS by GitHub Pages.
  • In the published build the app makes no network request at all once loaded. The optional AI endpoints described below are configured at build time and are not configured in the published build.
  • You should protect your device with a screen lock and your browser profile with a password, since those are what stand between someone else and your study data.

Data Sharing

Sharing With Other People

  • QuizCraftQA has no accounts, no pairing, no sharing and no collaboration features. There is nobody to share with and no way to.

Third Parties

  • No quiz/PDF content is shared with third parties by Sudarshan Tech Labs, except through the optional AI endpoints described below when a build configures them and you use the feature.
  • AI Assistance is off by default. The "Generate Study Plan" and question-variant buttons appear only after you turn it on in Settings.
  • Study plan: if the app build has a study-plan endpoint configured and you tap "Generate Study Plan", the app sends one request to that endpoint with your overall accuracy, summaries of your last 5 quiz sessions (question count, accuracy, flagged question count and IDs, a random session ID and end time) and per-topic statistics (section titles and numbers taken from your PDFs, answer, correct and flagged counts, accuracy, confidence and difficulty counts, and syllabus reference links). Question text, your answers and PDF text are not included.
  • Question variants: if the app build has a question-variant endpoint configured and you request a variant, the app sends that question's text, explanation, correct answer and a short passage from your PDF to that endpoint.
  • Requests are sent only when you tap the button. They carry no name, email, account or authorization header. If no endpoint is configured, no request is sent and the result is built on your device.
  • The endpoint is run by whoever configured that build, and that operator handles the data it receives. The published QuizCraftQA build configures neither endpoint, so in the version at the project's own URL both features fall back to results computed on your device and no request leaves your browser.
  • Links in the app to istqb.org and to the project's GitHub pages open in your browser and are requests you chose to make, the same as any other link.

We do not share your data with:

  • Data brokers
  • Unrelated advertising networks, unless advertising is explicitly listed above for this app
  • Third parties unrelated to the app features described in this policy

Permissions Used

Required Permissions

  • Browser storage: Used to save your local study history, imported PDF text and settings in IndexedDB.
  • Local file/PDF access through the browser file picker: Used only for the files you pick, and only to read them in your browser.

QuizCraftQA is a web app. It asks for no browser permission prompts — no camera, microphone, location or notifications — and it is not an Android app, so it has no Android permissions.

Optional Permissions

  • Installing the app to your home screen, and importing a PDF, are both optional. Using a private or temporary browser window keeps nothing after you close it.

Your Rights and Controls

Location Sharing Control

  • No location sharing exists in QuizCraftQA.

Account Management

  • There is no account to manage.

Data Access

  • Everything the app stores is in your own browser, under your control, and can be cleared from the app or from your browser's site settings at any time.

Clearing Browser Storage

  • Clear the site's data in your browser's site settings to remove the IndexedDB study data and the cached app files in one step.
  • Uninstalling the installed app, or closing a private window, has the same effect for that profile.

Children's Privacy

  • We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information, please contact us to request deletion.
  • Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of apps that store personal content.

Security

  • Local data is protected by your browser's same-origin policy and your operating system's own access controls. QuizCraftQA adds no encryption of its own.
  • The published app is served over HTTPS and, once loaded, makes no network request.
  • Third-party services named in this policy provide their own security controls and privacy policies.
  • You are responsible for protecting your device and your browser profile.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We never receive it.

Data Deletion

How to Delete Your Account

There is no account. Everything the app keeps is in your own browser, so deletion is something you do yourself:

Option 1: In-App Deletion

  1. Open QuizCraftQA
  2. Remove imported PDFs, history entries or settings where the app provides controls
  3. Turning the Telemetry toggle off clears the in-app event log immediately

Option 2: Clear the Site's Browser Storage

  1. Open your browser's site settings for the QuizCraftQA page
  2. Clear the site's data, which removes the IndexedDB keys quizcraftqa-uploaded-pdfs, quizcraftqa-question-history and quizcraftqa-settings and the cached app files
  3. If you installed the app to your home screen, uninstalling it removes its storage for that profile

Option 3: Email Deletion Request

Send an email to: support@sudarshantechlabs.com or sunny.sudarshan@gmail.com

  • Subject: "Data Deletion Request - QuizCraftQA"
  • We will answer honestly that we hold nothing of yours, because the app sends us nothing, and confirm that in writing if you need it for a record

What Gets Deleted

  • The three IndexedDB keys above: imported PDF text, question history and settings
  • The service worker's cache of the app's own files
  • Nothing on our side, because there is nothing on our side

Data Retention After Deletion

  • Immediate: clearing the site's data removes all of it at once
  • Private window: nothing survives closing it
  • AI requests already sent: if a build you used had an AI endpoint configured, whatever it received is held by that endpoint's operator, not by us, so a deletion request for it goes to them

Third-Party Services

QuizCraftQA uses none in its published build. No Firebase, no Google service, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no backend of ours. The app's PDF reader and charts are bundled into the page rather than loaded from a CDN, so no third party sees a request when you open it.

Earlier versions of this policy named Firebase as a security control protecting your data and linked the Firebase, Google and Google Play privacy policies. There is no Firebase and no Google service in this app — the string appears nowhere in its source. Those claims were wrong and have been removed.

A build that someone configures with an AI endpoint would add that endpoint's operator as a third party; the published build configures none.

The only third-party policy with any bearing on the published app is GitHub's, because GitHub Pages serves it:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement

About QuizCraftQA

QuizCraftQA is built with privacy in mind. The app is built with:

  • React and Vite, as a static site served by GitHub Pages
  • pdf.js, bundled and run in a web worker in your browser, for reading your PDFs
  • IndexedDB for your study data, through idb-keyval
  • An installable progressive web app shell, so it works offline
  • Optional AI endpoints, configured at build time and configured in no published build

Your PDFs and your answers stay in your browser. In the published build nothing leaves it at all.
